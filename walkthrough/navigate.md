## Move around the drawing

- Drag with the mouse to pan. Scroll to zoom at the cursor. Press F or double-click to fit the whole drawing.
- Untick a layer in the side panel to hide it. Layers that are off or frozen in the file start hidden.
- Press M, then click two points to measure. Points snap to line ends, arc ends, centres and vertices.
- Press B to switch between a dark and a white background.
