## DWG is not supported

DWG is a closed binary format. DXF Viewer opens DXF only, and shows a short page when you click a .dwg file.

To get a DXF file:

- AutoCAD, AutoCAD LT or DWG TrueView: Save As, then pick an AutoCAD DXF type.
- BricsCAD, ZWCAD or DraftSight: Save As, then pick DXF.
- LibreCAD, QCAD or FreeCAD: open the DWG and save it as DXF.
- The free ODA File Converter converts a whole folder at once.
